THE RULES ARE SIMPLE
01

Find the pole

Each end wall has one marked rectangular brick. Hit it with the ball to score. The rest of the wall sends the ball back.

02

Keep the ball

The ball stays at your feet while you have possession. S passes to a teammate; when defending, hold S to press and D to shove for a tackle. A shoots and E sprints.

03

Own the court

Play 1v1, 2v2, or 3v3 on a dusty enclosed court with brick walls, neighbourhood graffiti, and the occasional bit of litter.
